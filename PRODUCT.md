# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, senior engineers, and recruiters at Netherlands/Germany tech companies (Picnic, Adyen, Booking, Bol, Mollie, Zalando…) doing a 30–90 second credibility skim after seeing a LinkedIn post, CV, or GitHub link. Secondary: engineers who click through from a blog post or repo. They are screening for: is this person's "production ML" claim real, and is this a senior-shaped mind?

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of MD Minhazur Rahman — ML engineer + automation specialist (Chattogram, BD → relocating NL/DE, visa sponsorship). The site's one job: convert a skim into "this claim is verifiable" — every metric is baseline-anchored and links to the run/repo that produced it, and two live services can be exercised immediately. Success = recruiter opens a demo, repo, or the CV; ideal = replies to outreach.

## Brand Personality

Measured · engineered · candid. The voice of a careful engineer who publishes evidence, not adjectives. Confident without hype; "proof, not promises." Emotional goal: quiet conviction — the reader should feel they are reading a well-documented dossier about a rigorous person, not a pitch.

## Anti-references

- Dark "terminal cosplay" dev portfolios (the previous version of this very site — retired for being generic).
- SaaS-template landing pages: hero-metric blocks, identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism.
- The saturated 2026 "editorial-typographic" fingerprint: italic display serif + tiny tracked mono eyebrows + three rule-separated columns + zero imagery.
- Anything cute or mascot-like (a mascot was removed by the owner once; never returns).
- Fabricated or unanchored numbers. "95% accuracy" with no baseline is brand poison.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence is the aesthetic.** Metrics set like cited pull-quotes with visible sources; footnote/figure grammar instead of decorative scaffolding.
2. **Documented, not decorated.** Every visual device should look like it carries information (captions, indexes, margin notes) even when it delights.
3. **The person is real.** Photographic headshot treated editorially — this portfolio has a face, unlike the anonymous-dev default.
4. **One committed color, used with conviction.** No timid neutral-plus-nothing palettes.
5. **A 30-second skim always lands.** Bold moves never bury the hierarchy: name → claim → proof → contact must survive any scroll speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body contrast ≥4.5:1, focus-visible on all interactive elements, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation, semantic landmarks/headings, keyboard-complete. No hover-only affordances.
