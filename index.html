These days he works alongside me in Chattogram, where he's traded stalking prey for stalking anomalies. He reviews every model before it ships — and he has vetoed more than one.

The personality. Calm, patient, quietly confident — and completely allergic to hype. He doesn't believe a metric until he's seen the run that produced it, and he never trusts a model that hasn't beaten an honest baseline. If a forecast looks too good, Bagh assumes it's leakage.
